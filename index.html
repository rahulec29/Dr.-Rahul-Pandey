General Instructions

  1. You must enter the correct Section Code given by your instructor to unlock the Start Test button.
  2. The test consists of 12 questions across three sections — Section A: Basic (7 × 1 mark = 7), Section B: Intermediate (2 × 2 marks = 4), and Section C: Advanced (3 × 3 marks = 9) — for a total of 20 marks.
  3. Total duration: 60 minutes. The test will auto-submit when the timer reaches zero.
  4. There is 25% negative marking on wrong answers in Section A (Basic) and Section B (Intermediate). Section C (Advanced) has no negative marking.
  5. Each multiple-choice question has exactly one correct option. Questions requiring a typed answer must be entered exactly as instructed.
  6. Use Save & Next to save your answer and move to the next question.
  7. Use Mark for Review & Next if you want to revisit a question later — it will still count if you also answered it.
  8. Use Clear Response to deselect your chosen option for the current question.
  9. You may navigate to any question at any time using the question palette on the right.
  10. Click Submit Test at any time to end the test early, or let it auto-submit at time-up.
  11. The order of questions and the order of options within each MCQ are randomized separately for every candidate — your Question 1 may not be the same as someone else's Question 1.
  12. Your score is shown immediately after you submit, but the correct-answer review stays hidden until your test's time window is over (i.e. it unlocks at the same moment an auto-submit would happen, even if you finish early) — this keeps the answers from spreading to candidates who are still testing.
  13. Do not refresh or close this browser tab — your progress is saved automatically in this browser, but a stable internet connection is required at the time of final submission.
  14. Security monitoring: this test requires fullscreen mode. Switching tabs, minimizing the window, switching to another application, or exiting fullscreen will be detected. You will have a short countdown (5 seconds) to return — if you don't, your test will be automatically submitted at that moment.
  15. Copy, cut, paste, right-click, printing, and browser developer tools are disabled during the test.
  16. A live photo of you is captured before the test starts, and periodic snapshots may be captured during the test, for identity verification by your instructor.
  17. Please note (in fairness to you): this is browser-based monitoring, not a full lockdown system. It reliably detects tab/window switching on this device and disables in-page copying. It cannot prevent someone from photographing your screen with a separate phone/camera, nor guarantee against every possible screen-recording tool — your instructor may combine this with in-person invigilation.
